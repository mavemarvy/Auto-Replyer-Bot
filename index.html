🚀I’m new here. How do I get started?Complete setup from zero
  1. Open @Auto_replyerbot and send /start.
  2. Tap Open My Dashboard.
  3. Create your private 6–12 digit dashboard PIN.
  4. In Telegram, open Settings → Chat Automation and connect @Auto_replyerbot.
  5. Return to the dashboard and confirm that Profile connected says Connected.
  6. Create your first auto-reply rule and leave Auto reply switched ON.

Your first 30 days include the full Pro feature set.

🔌How do I connect the bot to my Telegram chats?Required before replies can work

Open Telegram Settings → Chat Automation, choose the connected bot/chatbot option, and connect @Auto_replyerbot. Telegram controls which chats the bot may manage. After connecting, reopen or refresh the dashboard.

💬How do I create a normal text auto-reply?Keyword → automatic message
  1. Choose a trigger such as Exact message or Contains word/text.
  2. Enter the incoming word or message.
  3. Choose Text message as the reply type.
  4. Type the reply the customer should receive.
  5. Tap Add rule.

Example: trigger price using Contains → reply Hello, here is the current price….

🧠What do Exact, Contains, Starts With and Ends With mean?Text matching explained

Exact only matches the full message. Contains matches when your saved text appears anywhere in the message. Starts With checks the beginning. Ends With checks the end. Contains is usually best when customers may add extra words.

⚡What does Priority mean?Which rule runs first

Lower numbers run first. Priority 10 is checked before 100, and 100 before 1000. If two rules could match the same message, the first matching rule wins. Put very specific rules above broad fallback rules.

Example: product keyword = 100, New Messenger = 1000, Default fallback = 5000.

👋How does New Messenger work?Reply to a person’s first message

A New Messenger rule runs when Auto Replyer Bot has not seen that customer before for your connected account. If you want to test it again with an old customer, use Reset seen people in Maintenance.

🔗How do I make “Reply A → customer replies anything → Reply B”?Conversation flow automation
  1. Create Reply B as a rule with trigger Flow step — only after another rule.
  2. Create or edit Reply A.
  3. Under After this reply, wait for the customer’s next message and send, choose Reply B.
  4. Save Reply A.

After Reply A is sent, the customer may type anything. Their next message triggers Reply B. You can chain B → C → D the same way.

🎙️How do I save a voice note, photo, video or file for auto-reply?Upload media through the bot
  1. Open your private chat with @Auto_replyerbot.
  2. Send or forward the voice note, photo, video, audio or document.
  3. Then send /upload Your Media Name.
  4. Refresh the dashboard. It will appear in My Media Library.

Example: /upload Welcome Voice. Pro currently supports up to 5 saved media items for public users.

🎧What is the difference between Voice and Audio?Telegram voice note vs music/audio file

Voice sends the familiar Telegram voice-note bubble. Audio sends a normal audio/music-style file. Save and select the matching media type in your Media Library.

🖼️Can the bot react when someone sends a photo, video, PDF or file?Media triggers

Yes on Pro. In the Trigger menu choose Photo/image, Video, PDF document, Any document/file, Voice note, Audio, or Any media, then choose the response.

✅How does payment-proof or media verification work?Approve or reject customer media
  1. Edit the rule that asks the customer to send proof.
  2. Enable Wait for the customer’s next media and send it to my bot chat for review.
  3. Add optional messages for Approve and Reject.
  4. When the customer sends their next media, it is forwarded to your private bot chat.
  5. Tap Approve or Reject. The customer receives your configured result message.
📚What bot commands can I use?Useful shortcuts

/start account setup · /dashboard open dashboard · /library list media · /upload Name save last media · /cancel clear pending upload · /resetpin reset dashboard PIN · /help bot help · /terms subscription terms · /support your message general support · /paysupport your message payment support.

🔐I forgot my dashboard PIN. What do I do?Secure PIN reset

Open your own private chat with @Auto_replyerbot and send /resetpin. Then reopen the dashboard and create a new 6–12 digit PIN. Do not share your PIN with anyone.

🎁How does the referral reward work?Earn free Pro hours

Copy your referral link from the dashboard. A referral becomes qualified after the referred person creates their account/PIN and connects Auto Replyer Bot. Every 2 qualified referrals earns 1 hour of full Pro access. Earned hours remain saved until your normal paid/trial access is inactive, then you can activate them one hour at a time.

⭐How do subscriptions and Telegram Stars work?Basic, Pro and renewal

New users get a 30-day full Pro trial. Basic is for normal text auto-replies. Pro adds flows, media replies, media triggers and verification. In-Telegram digital subscriptions use Telegram Stars and renew every 30 days until renewal is canceled.

You may subscribe before the trial ends; if you do, the paid 30-day period starts immediately.

🟢Why is my auto-reply not working?Quick troubleshooting checklist
  1. Make sure Auto reply is ON.
  2. Check that Profile connected says Connected.
  3. Make sure the rule itself is Enabled.
  4. Check its Priority against other matching rules.
  5. If using Basic, remember flows/media/verification require Pro.
  6. If your trial/subscription expired, activate a subscription or referral hour.
  7. For Contains rules, save a useful phrase rather than an unnecessarily long exact paragraph.
🔄How do I cancel or resume subscription renewal?Keep access until paid period ends

When a recurring Stars subscription is active, the dashboard shows a subscription-management card. Tap Cancel renewal to stop the next automatic charge. Your current paid access stays active until its end date. You can re-enable renewal from the same card while Telegram allows it.

🧹What does “Reset seen people” do?New-messenger testing

It clears your bot’s memory of which customer chats have already been seen. This is mainly useful when testing a New Messenger rule. It does not delete your rules, media or reply history.

🆘I still need help. How do I contact support?General and payment help

In your private bot chat, send /support your question for general assistance or /paysupport your payment issue for subscription/payment support. Your request is recorded as a support ticket.
